# news-image-01 — The World in One Day

**Competition:** [make.fun — Sum up 13 November's world news in one image](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-01) (`news-image-01`, $250 total, top 100 share the pool).

**Entry:** `main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies `entry.png` to
`/output/entry.png`. The image is a 1024×1024 PNG (782 KB) newspaper front
page, "THE WORLD IN ONE DAY — Friday, 13 November 2026". It was composed by
`compose.py` (PIL) from four AI-generated editorial illustrations in
`assets/`; all headlines and text are rendered by the composition script, so
the program is deterministic and runs with no network.

## The stories in the image

All stories were reported on Friday 13 November 2026.

1. **The Gulf — Tankers hit, radar down as Iran war escalates (hero).**
   Iran's Revolutionary Guards said they destroyed a US early-warning radar
   in Bahrain; an LNG tanker struck a naval mine in the Strait of Hormuz
   and four tankers were hit in two days. President Trump said "We don't
   need a deal with Iran" and pulled Vice President JD Vance from peace
   talks. Sources: Just Security Early Edition, Zark News, Al Jazeera.
2. **Europe — EU wins a seat at Ukraine peace talks.** Russia and Ukraine
   agreed to bring European delegates into the next round of peace talks,
   announced by US envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner after meetings
   with Putin and Zelenskyy; work begins on the war's largest prisoner
   exchange. Sources: AP via Bangladesh Chronicle, Zark News, SatRecon.
3. **Technology — Microsoft outage hits Outlook.** A global Microsoft 365
   outage took Outlook, Teams and Copilot offline; Microsoft said it was
   not a cybersecurity incident, but fixes were still largely failing more
   than 12 hours in. Sources: Al Jazeera via SatRecon, Open Chronicle.
4. **Business — Nvidia chief Huang resigns, summoned by Congress.**
   Nvidia founder Jensen Huang resigned as chief executive and from the
   board (CFO Colette Kress became interim CEO) and was summoned to
   testify before Congress about an alleged affair with an alleged Chinese
   spy. Sources: BBC via SatRecon, Bangladesh Chronicle, Open Chronicle.
5. **Also today.** Pope Leo XIV met six clergy-abuse survivors in Peru as
   record crowds filled Lima (France 24 via SatRecon); the Brooklyn
   building collapse toll settled at 8 dead, 18 injured (Zark News); the
   US Justice Department won court approval to seize $1.2B in crypto
   stolen from Binance in a hack the FBI attributes to North Korea's
   TraderTraitor unit (Zark News).
